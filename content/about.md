# About Professor K M Shaw

Professor K M Shaw is an Emeritus Professor of Medicine at the University of Portsmouth and Honorary Consultant Physician at Portsmouth Hospitals NHS Trust.

## Career Highlights

Professor Shaw has had a distinguished career in medicine, particularly in the field of diabetes and endocrinology. His career spans several decades, during which he has made significant contributions to research, clinical practice, and medical education.

### Academic Achievements

- MA (Cantab) from Downing College, Cambridge
- MD FRCP
- Director of Research and Development at Portsmouth Hospitals NHS Trust (1995-2008)
- Scientific Fellow of the Zoological Society of London
- External Examiner for multiple universities

### Professional Leadership

Professor Shaw has held numerous leadership positions in professional organizations:

- Founding Member and Trustee of the Association of British Clinical Diabetologists
- Member of the Professional Advisory Council for Diabetes UK
- Editor-in-Chief of Practical Diabetes International for over two decades
- Member of various committees at the Royal College of Physicians

## Research Focus

His research interests include:

- Clinical and biochemical determinants of early nephropathy in Type 1 Diabetes
- Cardiovascular risk factors
- Markers of endothelial dysfunction in diabetic vasculopathy
- Pharmacological evaluation of novel therapeutic agents in diabetes
- 18th century naval medicine

## Personal Philosophy

Throughout his career, Professor Shaw has been dedicated to improving the lives of people with diabetes through clinical care, research, and education. He believes in a patient-centered approach to medicine and the importance of evidence-based practice.

## Legacy

Professor Shaw's contributions to the field of diabetes and endocrinology continue to influence clinical practice and research. His work has helped shape policies and guidelines for the management of diabetes in the UK and beyond.
