# Publications by Professor K M Shaw

Professor Shaw has published extensively in the field of diabetes and endocrinology throughout his distinguished career. His research has contributed significantly to our understanding of diabetic complications, particularly nephropathy and cardiovascular disease.

Below you will find a selection of his key publications, organized by topic area. Each publication includes a brief summary and, where available, a link to the full text.

## Key Research Areas

- Clinical and biochemical determinants of early nephropathy in Type 1 Diabetes
- Cardiovascular risk factors in diabetes
- Pharmacological evaluation of novel therapeutic agents
- 18th century naval medicine
